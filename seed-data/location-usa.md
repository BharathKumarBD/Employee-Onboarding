# Acme Corp USA — Location-Specific Onboarding Guide

> This document applies to employees based in the United States.

## Federal Public Holidays (USA)

US employees observe federal public holidays:
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (third Monday of January)
- Presidents' Day (third Monday of February)
- Memorial Day (last Monday of May)
- Juneteenth (June 19)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (first Monday of September)
- Columbus Day / Indigenous Peoples' Day (second Monday of October)
- Veterans Day (November 11)
- Thanksgiving Day (fourth Thursday of November)
- Christmas Day (December 25)

Additional state holidays may apply. Check the HR portal for the full USA holiday calendar.

## Benefits (USA)

### Health Insurance
- Acme Corp USA employees are eligible for health insurance from Day 1.
- Must enroll within 30 days of start date.
- Plans: Basic PPO (employer covers 80%) and Premium PPO with dental and vision (employer covers 70%).
- Contact benefits-usa@acmecorp.com for enrollment forms.
- HSA (Health Savings Account) is available with the high-deductible plan option.

### 401(k) Retirement Plan
- Acme Corp matches up to 4% of salary after 6 months of employment.
- Auto-enrolled at 3% contribution after first paycheck; adjust in the HR portal.
- Roth 401(k) option available.
- Vesting schedule: 25% per year, fully vested after 4 years.

### Dental and Vision
- Standalone dental and vision plans available if not on Premium PPO.
- Delta Dental network; VSP for vision.

### Life and Disability Insurance
- Basic life insurance (1× annual salary) provided at no cost.
- Short-term and long-term disability coverage available.

## Legal and Compliance Requirements (USA)

### I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification
- Must be completed within **3 business days** of your start date.
- Bring acceptable identity and work authorization documents (passport, driver's license + Social Security card, etc.).
- HR will walk you through the I-9 process during Day 1.

### W-4 Tax Withholding
- Complete your federal W-4 form on Day 1 through the HR portal.
- Also complete state W-4 if applicable for your state.

### Social Security Number
- Required for payroll; submit to Payroll team during onboarding.

## Leave Policy (USA)

In addition to global PTO (25 days/year):
- **FMLA**: Up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year (eligible after 12 months employment, 50+ employee worksite).
- **Sick Leave**: Varies by state — check HR for your state's requirements.
- **Parental Leave**: Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks paid; secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks paid.
- **Bereavement Leave**: 5 days paid for immediate family.

## State-Specific Notes

- **California employees**: Subject to California-specific employment laws including additional meal/rest break requirements, California SDI, and CFRA (California Family Rights Act up to 12 weeks).
- **New York employees**: New York Paid Family Leave (PFL) contributions deducted from paycheck; NY FMLA supplements federal FMLA.
- For other states, HR will advise on any additional state-specific requirements.

## Onboarding Requirements (USA-Specific)

1. Complete I-9 form within 3 business days of start date.
2. Submit W-4 and state withholding forms.
3. Enroll in health insurance within 30 days.
4. Set up direct deposit in the HR portal.
5. Register for 401(k) (auto-enrolled; can adjust contribution).
