# Dayforce India — Location-Specific Onboarding Guide

> This document applies to employees based in India (offices in Bangalore, Mumbai, and remote India locations).

## Public Holidays (India)

Indian employees observe national public holidays including:
- Republic Day (January 26)
- Independence Day (August 15)
- Gandhi Jayanti (October 2)
- Diwali (date varies — check HR portal each year)
- Holi (date varies — check HR portal)
- Eid al-Fitr (date varies — check HR portal)

Additional regional/state holidays may apply based on your office location. The full holiday calendar is available on the HR portal under "India Holidays."

## Statutory Benefits (India)

### Provident Fund (PF)
- All employees earning below ₹15,000/month basic salary are covered under EPF (Employees' Provident Fund) mandatorily.
- Employees above this threshold may opt in voluntarily.
- Employer contributes 12% of basic salary to EPF.
- Complete PF enrollment form with HR within your first week.

### ESIC (Employees' State Insurance)
- Applicable if your gross salary is ₹21,000/month or below.
- Provides medical and sickness benefits through the ESIC scheme.
- HR will advise if you are eligible during onboarding.

### Gratuity
- Employees become eligible for gratuity after 5 years of continuous service.
- Gratuity is calculated as: 15 days' wages × years of service.

### Professional Tax
- Professional Tax is deducted monthly from salary based on your state of employment.
- Bangalore (Karnataka): up to ₹200/month. Mumbai (Maharashtra): up to ₹200/month.

## Leave Policy (India)

In addition to Dayforce's global PTO policy, India employees receive:
- **Earned Leave (EL)**: 15 days per year (accrued monthly), carries over up to 30 days.
- **Sick Leave (SL)**: 12 days per year.
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: 12 days per year.
- **Maternity Leave**: 26 weeks paid for first two children (as per Maternity Benefit Act).
- **Paternity Leave**: 5 days paid.

## Onboarding Requirements (India-Specific)

1. Submit PAN card copy to Payroll within Day 1.
2. Submit Aadhaar card copy for identity verification.
3. Provide bank account details for salary processing (HDFC, ICICI, SBI preferred for faster processing).
4. Complete PF nomination form (Form 2) within first week.
5. If relocating, submit rent receipts for HRA tax exemption.

## IT and Connectivity (India)

- VPN connection may experience higher latency from India — contact IT if connections are slow.
- Use `it-help@dayforce.com` or Slack `#it-support` for assistance.
- Recommended: Use office Wi-Fi or high-speed broadband (minimum 50 Mbps) for video calls.
