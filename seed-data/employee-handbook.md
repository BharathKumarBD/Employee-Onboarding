# Dayforce Employee Handbook

## Remote Work Policy

Dayforce supports a hybrid work model. Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**, including Fridays. Remote work must be coordinated with your manager and logged in the HR portal by the prior Wednesday.

Core collaboration hours are **10:00–15:00 EET** on remote days. Employees must be available on Slack during these hours.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees receive **25 days of PTO per year**, accruing monthly. PTO requests require manager approval at least **5 business days** in advance for absences longer than 2 days.

Unused PTO above 5 days does not roll over to the next calendar year.

## Code of Conduct

All employees must treat colleagues with respect, maintain confidentiality of company information, and report conflicts of interest to HR. Harassment of any kind is grounds for immediate termination.

## Office Locations

Headquarters: Minneapolis, Minnesota, United States.

## Direct Deposit & Payroll Setup

New employees must set up direct deposit within their first week:
1. Log in to the Aarambh-AI portal or contact HR.
2. Provide your bank name and the **last 4 digits** of your account number (full account numbers are never stored).
3. Provide your routing number (US) or IFSC code (India).
4. Select your tax filing status (W-4 for US, New/Old Regime for India, EU Standard for Europe).
5. HR will verify your details within 2 business days.
6. First payroll will be processed on the next regular pay cycle after verification.

For questions, contact payroll@dayforce.com.
